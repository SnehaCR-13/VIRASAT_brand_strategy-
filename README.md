# VIRASAT – Brand Strategy

## Project Overview

VIRASAT is a group project focused on developing a contemporary Indian luxury fashion brand that blends traditional Indian artisan craftsmanship with modern design and streetwear. The concept aims to preserve India's textile heritage while making it relevant and reachable to global audiences.

## Project Focus

The project explores:

- Brand story, vision and mission
- Problem identification and proposed solution
- Brand identity, values and aesthetic
- Traditional craft-based collections
- Target audience and market positioning
- Artisan empowerment and sustainable fashion
- Digital marketing and social media strategy
- Campaign storytelling and brand promotion
- Future growth and expansion opportunities

VIRASAT draws inspiration from traditional crafts including Bandhani, Kasuti, Jamdani, Kantha, Ajrakh and Sambalpuri Ikat, reimagining them through contemporary fashion.

## Digital Marketing Strategy

The project incorporates influencer collaborations, paid advertising, user-generated content, hashtag campaigns, social media launches and editorial storytelling to build brand awareness and engagement.
